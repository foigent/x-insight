# Code of Conduct

Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and evidence without attacking the person. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted contact, and publication of someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Be honest about evidence and uncertainty. Respect other people's time, privacy, and access boundaries. Chinese discussion is welcome; language fluency is not a measure of contribution quality.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or decline contributions to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

There is currently no separately published private conduct-reporting contact. Ask maintainers for a private route without posting personal details in public. Do not assume a security reporting channel also accepts conduct reports. Once a route is agreed, share only what is needed to assess the behavior.
